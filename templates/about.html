About AgriRent

Helping farms find the tools to grow.

A simpler way for farmers and local equipment owners to connect around the work that matters.

Local equipment. Clear expectations.

AgriRent helps farmers find locally available tractors and agricultural equipment. Owners publish their machines, services, prices and available working hours. Farmers can request work and follow booking and payment status.

A demo built for learning

This beginner-friendly application is a local demo. Before real transactions, deploy behind HTTPS, configure a persistent secret key, review privacy and legal requirements, and integrate a verified payment provider.
